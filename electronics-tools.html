⚡ Electronics Tools

Three small calculators for electrical and electronic engineering students. You can type values with prefixes, like 4.7k, 10m or 4k7.

Ohm's Law Calculator

Fill in any two values and leave the third empty. The calculator finds it and also gives the power.

Resistor Colour Code

Pick the colour of each band, reading from the end with the bands closest together.

Series & Parallel Resistors
